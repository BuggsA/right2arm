CCW/HGP Class

The full carry permit course: classroom, then the range on Day 2.

$275+ range fee, due on Day 2

The complete course for your carry permit. You learn in the classroom, then put it into practice with live fire at the range on Day 2. Firearms, ammo and safety gear are provided, so you do not need to own a firearm to take this class.

What's included

  • Safety-first instruction
  • Safety equipment: eye, ear and glove protection
  • Firearms to train with
  • Ammo for all live-fire appointments
  • Targets
  • First cleaning materials (post-purchase class)
  • A walkthrough of the pre- and post-purchase paperwork and state/federal filings

Good to know

  • The range fee is paid separately on Day 2.
