# Photo credits (concept 2)

These are **stand-in photos** that show the look of the site. Swap them for the client's own photos of his classes, students and range before launch.

All photos are from [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com) and free to use under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license): commercial use is allowed and credit isn't required, but it's listed here anyway. They're different photos from concept 1.

Every photo on this site is toned black, orange and cream (a "duotone") to match the logo. That treatment is what makes mixed photos look like one set, so apply it to the client's photos too. It can be done in any photo editor with a gradient map from `#16120e` (shadows) through `#c07c34` (midtones) to `#f7ebd9` (highlights). Save the result as WebP, about 900px wide for the class photos and 1800px for `group.webp`.
